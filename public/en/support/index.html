Which files can it read?

Trial balances (mizan) exported from accounting software (Excel .xlsx, CSV, PDF), financial statements published on KAP, and side-by-side balance sheet tables. Older Excel files (.xls) can be opened in Excel and saved as .xlsx. The app interface is currently in Turkish and is built for the Turkish uniform chart of accounts.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed only through the Apple App Store or Google Play. On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Bilanly. On Android: Google Play > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

My subscription does not appear.

Open the PRO screen in the app and tap "Satın alımları geri yükle" (Restore Purchases). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

I would like a refund.

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, or "Report a problem" in your Google Play order history.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Ayarlar (Settings) > Veriler ve yedek (Data and backup) > "Bu cihazdaki bütün verileri sil" (Delete all data on this device). Deleting the app also removes everything. There is no account, so the developer holds no data about you.
